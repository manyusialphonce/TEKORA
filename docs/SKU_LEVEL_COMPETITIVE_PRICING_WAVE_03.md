# TEKORA SKU-Level Competitive Pricing — Wave 03 (Categories 10–22)
**Snapshot:** 26 November 2026 · **Status:** Provisional, quote-based and not approved.

## 10 — Payments, Billing & Fintech
SKU: PAY-API (transaction); PAY-MOBILE-MONEY (percent/fixed); PAY-CARD (%+fee); PAY-QR; PAY-PAYOUT; PAY-REFUND; PAY-RECONCILIATION (account/month); PAY-CONTROL-NUMBER; PAY-BILLING (tenant/month); PAY-SANDBOX. Public benchmark from prior research: BLMPay business tier mobile money 3%, cards 3.5%, QR 2.5%; payout fees separately (https://pay.blmtec.co.tz/). Sandbox only. Control number and live processing BLOCKED pending legal classification, licensed provider contract, settlement, custody, BoT review, tax and approvals. No real funds.

## 11 — Developer Tools & API
SKU: API-FREE (project quota); API-REQUEST (1k/1M); API-WEBHOOK (event); API-TEAM (seat/project); API-LOGS (GB+retention); API-EDGE (compute); API-SUPPORT (tier); API-SANDBOX. Freemium hard limits; paid by request, compute, egress, retention or seats. Rate limits, budgets, idempotency and abuse controls. Explorer/sandbox free, costly production metered.

## 12 — Software Development, DevOps & Deployment
SKU: DEV-DISCOVERY (fixed); DEV-ENGINEER (hour/day); DEV-MILESTONE (deliverable); DEV-CI (runner-minute); DEV-DEPLOY (environment/month); DEV-MIGRATION (project); DEV-MANAGED-DEVOPS (retainer); DEV-INCIDENT (incident/hour). Quote by scope, acceptance, IP, change control, warranty, SLA and staffing. Bounded discovery/checklist may be free; engineering not unlimited.

## 13 — Cybersecurity
SKU: SEC-ASSESS (scope); SEC-VULN-SCAN (asset/month); SEC-PENTEST (authorized engagement); SEC-MONITOR (endpoint/log GB); SEC-INCIDENT (retainer/hour); SEC-TRAINING (seat); SEC-HARDEN (project); SEC-COMPLIANCE (readiness). Quote-only pending authorization, qualifications, liability, evidence handling, tooling, insurance and capacity. Free checklist, not unscoped testing or certification.

## 14 — Monitoring, Observability & SRE
SKU: OBS-HOST (host-hour); OBS-METRIC (samples/series); OBS-LOG (GB+retention); OBS-TRACE (spans); OBS-SYNTHETIC (check); OBS-ALERT (tier); SRE-ONCALL (coverage/month); SRE-REVIEW (project). Grafana Cloud publishes usage/free tiers; app observability references $0.025/host-hour and $19 platform fee in plan context, subject to terms (https://grafana.com/pricing/). Limited telemetry free, quotas/cardinality/retention safeguards; paid metered usage.

## 15 — AI, ML & Automation
SKU: AI-TEXT (model-specific input/output tokens); AI-EMBED (tokens/input); AI-IMAGE (image); AI-AUDIO (minute/token); AI-VIDEO (generated second); AI-GPU (GPU-second/hour); AI-VECTOR (GB/ops); AI-AGENT (task+tool); AI-AUTOMATION (run/seat); AI-SUPPORT. Provider/model-version registry, pass-through + disclosed orchestration fee, budgets and caps. Free sandbox credits with expiry. No universal token price or unlimited free use.

## 16 — Media, Video & Realtime
SKU: MEDIA-RTC (participant-minute); MEDIA-STREAM (viewer-minute); MEDIA-RECORD (minute); MEDIA-TRANSCODE (source minute/profile); MEDIA-STORAGE (GB-month); MEDIA-DELIVERY (GB egress); MEDIA-TURN (GB); MEDIA-MODERATION (usage). Cost separately for participant/viewer minutes, recording, transcoding, storage, delivery. Limited test sessions; validate concurrency, region, TURN, pipeline, retention.

## 17 — Data Engineering & Analytics
SKU: DATA-INGEST (GB/event); DATA-TRANSFORM (run/compute minute); DATA-WAREHOUSE (compute-hour); DATA-QUERY (TB scanned); DATA-STORAGE (GB-month); DATA-DASHBOARD (seat); DATA-PIPELINE (pipeline/month); DATA-ENGINEERING (project/day). Sandbox data and low quotas; meter compute, scans, ingest, storage, egress. Quote complex governance and engineering.

## 18 — Business Applications & Productivity
SKU: APP-BASE (seat/month); APP-MODULE (module/month); APP-WORKFLOW (run); APP-STORAGE (GB-month); APP-INTEGRATION (connector/month); APP-IMPLEMENTATION (project); APP-SUPPORT (tenant/month). Zoho public plans are regional/license dependent (https://www.zoho.com/workplace/plan-comparison.html). Free basic/pilot where sustainable; verify reseller rights, licensing, privacy and support.

## 19 — Managed IT & Consulting
SKU: IT-ASSESSMENT (engagement); IT-SERVICE-DESK (user/device/month); IT-MANAGED-SITE (site/month); IT-ONSITE (visit/day); IT-PROJECT (milestone); IT-RETAINER (month); IT-TRAINING (seat/day). Quote assets, geography, coverage, travel, staffing, SLA and liability. Short discovery bounded; no unstaffed 24/7 promise.

## 20 — IoT & Connected Infrastructure
SKU: IOT-DEVICE (device/month); IOT-CONNECTIVITY (SIM/MB/GB); IOT-EVENT (event); IOT-GATEWAY (device/month); IOT-STORAGE (GB-month); IOT-INSTALL (site/project); IOT-MANAGED (fleet/month); IOT-HARDWARE (unit). Simulator/test fleet only free. Hardware, connectivity and field work paid/quote; validate certification, operator coverage, lifecycle, security, warranty.

## 21 — Digital Trust, Compliance & Identity
SKU: ID-VERIFY (check); ID-AUTH (event); ID-OTP (channel/event); ID-SIGN (document); ID-CERT (certificate/year); ID-AUDIT (scope); ID-COMPLIANCE (assessment). Test identities only; no certification claim. Validate contracts, lawful basis, consent, retention, jurisdiction, privacy and regulator acceptance.

## 22 — Licensing, Distribution & Digital Products
SKU: LIC-SEAT (seat/month/year); LIC-SUBSCRIPTION; LIC-ACTIVATION; LIC-ADDON; LIC-SUPPORT (tenant/month); LIC-MIGRATION; DIGITAL-PRODUCT (license). Authorized reseller prices only; trials/open-source according to terms. Validate territory, authorization, renewals, tax, audit and license compliance.

## Shared approval requirements
Timestamped evidence, normalized units/specs, provider quote, FX/VAT, fully loaded cost (infra, egress, support, fraud, fees, overhead), margin floor, legal/commercial gate, quotas, paid conversion consent, versioned price book, invoice tests.
