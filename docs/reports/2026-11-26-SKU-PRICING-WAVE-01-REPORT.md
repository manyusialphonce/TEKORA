# TEKORA SKU-Level Competitive Pricing Analysis — Wave 01 Report

**Date:** 26 November 2026  
**Scope:** Categories 01 Domains/DNS, 02 Hosting/Compute and 09 SMS/WhatsApp.  
**Status:** PARTIAL — public competitor research and provisional SKU target hypotheses documented; no retail prices approved.

## Work completed

- Captured Tanzania-facing public .co.tz registration and renewal prices, plus .tz, .africa, .net and .sc.tz examples.
- Captured shared hosting and WordPress plan tiers with annual commitments and listed resource allowances.
- Captured Tanzania VPS resource/price anchors with vCPU, RAM, storage and monthly billing where publicly specified.
- Captured multiple Tanzania SMS volume ladders and WhatsApp price models, including cases where Meta fees are additional.
- Built provisional TEKORA target ranges for selected comparable domain, hosting, VPS and SMS SKUs.
- Defined SKU IDs, chargeable units, free-entry controls, renewal/segment safeguards and commercial approval gates.
- Preserved the remaining 19 categories in the all-22-category matrix for future waves.

## Principal public observations

- Hostraha shared hosting: TZS 56,999/year (25 GB SSD), TZS 94,999/year (50 GB SSD), TZS 139,999/year (100 GB SSD); applicable taxes excluded.
- Lineserve Linux VPS: 1 vCPU / 2 GB / 40 GB at TZS 32,400/month; 2 vCPU / 4 GB / 80 GB at TZS 64,800/month; higher tiers scale by published resource.
- Sakurahost .co.tz: TZS 19,500 registration and TZS 25,000 renewal; .tz TZS 100,000 registration/renewal on its public table.
- NextSMS Tanzania SMS: TZS 16 down to TZS 10.50 per SMS by monthly volume tier; sender ID registration required.
- DARSMS: TZS 22 to TZS 15 per SMS by volume tier, VAT excluded.
- SasiSMS: TZS 22 / 19 / 16 per SMS at published 1,000 / 10,000 / 100,000-credit levels.
- MasokoDigital: TZS 35 down to TZS 14 by prepaid volume; WhatsApp category rates published separately.
- WhatsApp providers disclose different billing models; one public TZS 12 price explicitly excludes Meta fees.

## Key decisions / safeguards

- The 3–8% undercut remains a conditional hypothesis, not a universal price rule.
- Proposed ranges are not approved prices. A supplier quote, cost model, tax/legal review and margin-floor approval are mandatory.
- Domain registration cannot be sold on the assumption of registrar authorization; verify TCRA/tzNIC accreditation or an authorized reseller arrangement.
- SMS must be billed by provider-defined segments, not assumed one SMS per message; Unicode and long-message segmentation need tests.
- WhatsApp customer pricing must separate Meta pass-through, BSP/provider fee, TEKORA platform fee and tax.
- Annual hosting must show renewal price, resource limits, backup scope, taxes and support terms.
- VPS comparisons must normalize CPU allocation, storage, traffic, IPv4, management, region and VAT.
